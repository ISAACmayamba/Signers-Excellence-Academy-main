About the ZSL Program

Our Zambian Sign Language course is structured to take you from absolute zero knowledge to confident visual-gestural communication. Unlike spoken languages, ZSL utilizes spatial location, movement, hand orientation, and facial expression as grammatical structures.

Classes are taught by certified Deaf instructors, ensuring an authentic immersion environment where students build spatial reasoning and expressive fluency from day one.

What You Will Master:

✅ Manual Alphabets & Numbers
✅ Facial Expression Syntax
✅ Deaf Culture & Etiquette
✅ Directional Verbs & Classifiers
✅ Emergency & Legal Vocabulary
✅ Real-time Conversational Practice

Syllabus Breakdown

01

Module 1: Foundations & Manual Communication

Fingerspelling alphabet, 1-100 numerals, personal pronouns, basic greetings, and introduction to Deaf culture principles.

02

Module 2: Family, Time & Spatial Placement

Establishing timelines (past/present/future), family relationships, calendar signs, and non-manual signals for questions.

03

Module 3: Community, Work & Classifiers

Navigating locations, professions, medical context, directional verbs, and using classifiers for size and shape descriptions.

04

Module 4: Advanced Dialogue & Assessment

Complex sentence structure, storytelling, handling fast dialogue, expressive video assignments, and final practical exam.
