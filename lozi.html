Master Conversational Silozi

Silozi is the main lingua franca of Western Zambia, spoken across Mongu, Sesheke, Senanga, Kaoma, and extending into parts of Namibia and Botswana. Characterized by its musical rhythm and distinct respectful posture in communication, learning Lozi is crucial for NGO field officers, administrative personnel, researchers, and tourism professionals.

Our program combines grammar foundations with cultural etiquette, preparing you to speak with confidence in both formal royal court settings (*Kuta*) and everyday community trade.

Key Learning Outcomes:

✅ Formal & Royal Respect Honorifics
✅ Essential Household & Market Terms
✅ Noun Classes & Verb Conjugations
✅ Field Deployment Vocab for NGOs
✅ Directional & Time Construction

Essential Lozi Expressions

Click any phrase to hear pronunciation guidance and practice along.

"Muzuweha cwani?"

English: How did you wake up? / Good morning

Usage: Traditional morning greeting used when addressing an individual or group with respect.

"Lumela!"

English: Hello! / Greetings

Usage: Standard general greeting appropriate at any time of day.

"Inkomoko / Ni itumezi"

English: Thank you very much

Usage: Expressing gratitude. Accompanied by soft clapping in traditional settings.

Syllabus Breakdown

01

Module 1: Greetings, Honorifics & Cultural Protocols

Mastering time-dependent greetings, royal honorifics, handclapping etiquette (*Kukambwela*), and introducing yourself.

02

Module 2: Sentence Structure & Noun Prefixes

Understanding Silozi noun class structures, pronouns, asking questions (*Muni?*, *Cwani?*, *Kae?*), and counting.

03

Module 3: Commerce, Travel & Community Life

Marketplace bargaining, transport directions, food and hospitality vocabulary, and health-related dialogue.

04

Module 4: Advanced Dialogue & Field Practicum

Extended conversations, translating documents, participating in simulated field interviews, and final oral evaluation.
