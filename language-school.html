Structure

Learning Pathways & Levels

Our language curriculum follows a structured 3-tier framework suited for every learning stage.

Ideal for absolute beginners with no prior exposure. You will learn:

  • Basic greetings, introductions, and cultural etiquette
  • Alphabet, numbers, and core vocabulary
  • Simple everyday sentence structures and common phrases
  • Confidence building through guided dialogue exercises

Designed for learners looking to communicate independently. Focus areas include:

  • Navigating marketplace conversations, transit, and directions
  • Expressing opinions, needs, and past/future events
  • Understanding regional idioms and conversational rhythms
  • Expanding active vocabulary and grammar accuracy

Tailored for field workers, public speakers, researchers, and aspiring interpreters:

  • Complex discussions on socio-economic, medical, and legal topics
  • Proverbs, storytelling, and deep cultural immersion
  • Fast-paced real-time translation and interpretation practice
  • Final oral assessment and certification presentation
